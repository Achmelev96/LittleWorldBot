Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information LittleWorldBot (“the Bot”) collects, why it is used, and the choices available to users.

1. Information we collect

The Bot stores only the identifiers needed to provide and operate its functionality:

  • Discord User IDs
  • Discord Guild (server) IDs

The Bot does not store Discord messages or message content.

2. How information is used

These identifiers are used only to operate the Bot, associate requests and settings with the correct user or Discord server, maintain service reliability, and prevent abuse.

3. Sharing of information

Stored information is not sold. It is not shared with third parties except when required by law or when necessary to protect the Bot, its users, or the service from misuse.

4. Data retention and deletion

Identifiers are retained only for as long as they are needed to provide the Bot’s functionality. To request deletion of data associated with your Discord user or server, contact @zoizan on Discord. Reasonable verification may be required before fulfilling a deletion request.

5. Third-party services

The Bot operates through Discord and may interact with music content providers. Your use of those services is also subject to their respective privacy policies and terms.

6. Children’s privacy

The Bot is not intended for anyone who is not permitted to use Discord under Discord’s own terms and applicable law.

7. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the Bot’s features or data practices change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when changes are made.

8. Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, contact @zoizan on Discord.
